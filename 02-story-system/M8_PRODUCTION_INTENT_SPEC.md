# M8 Production Intent Specification

**Version:** 1.0

Production intent derives episode and scene direction from one immutable approved M7 package. Required lineage is Story, Concept, Outline, Script/version, Review/version, Package/version, `canonVersion`, `canonRef`, source snapshot byte SHA-256, and exact per-scene source hashes.

Required content: episode visual language; scene dramatic objective, emotional turn, point of view and audience response; camera, lens, framing, movement and focus; playable performance; blocking/spatial continuity; lighting/atmosphere; transition/pacing; and stable artifact/scene/shot IDs. Every scene retains exact M7 `sourceText`. Production choices remain reversible and non-canon.

Film Director instructions are read in full and used before storyboard work unless a recorded simple-scene rationale permits a skip. No specialist may rewrite plot, dialogue, character intent, canon, or M7 meaning.
