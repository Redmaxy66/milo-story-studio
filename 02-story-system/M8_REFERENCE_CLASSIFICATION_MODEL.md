# M8 Reference Classification Model

**Version:** 1.0

- `CANON_FACT_DERIVATION`: directly traceable constraint from approved canon; may not extend or resolve an open decision.
- `APPROVED_PRODUCTION_CHOICE`: human-approved, reversible, scope-bound production treatment; never canon by implication.
- `APPROVED_NON_CANON_REFERENCE`: rights-cleared influence approved for declared uses; explicitly non-canon.

Classification precedes approval. `PENDING_REVIEW` entries are proposals even when they use one of these classification values. Conflicts, missing provenance, ambiguous rights, unsupported canon, or a proposed silent canon resolution block approval. Replacement, retirement, and supersession are append-versioned and preserve prior checksums and records.
