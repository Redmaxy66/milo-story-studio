# Milo Backstory

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved initial canon
**Designation date:** 18 July 2026

## Approved origin story

Milo was born when a bright star tumbled into Moonberry Wood and came to rest inside a glowing moonberry flower.

## Granny Bramble

Milo was raised by Granny Bramble.

## Meaning of the glowing star

The golden star on Milo's belly glows whenever someone needs courage, help, or hope.

## Milo's purpose

Milo's purpose is to follow curiosity, choose kindness, and bring light wherever it is needed.

This is Milo's in-world purpose within Moonberry Wood.

## Facts that must remain consistent

- Milo was born from a star that fell into Moonberry Wood and came to rest inside a glowing moonberry flower.
- Milo was raised by Granny Bramble.
- The golden star on Milo's belly glows in response to someone needing courage, help, or hope.
- Milo's purpose is to follow curiosity, choose kindness, and bring light wherever it is needed.
