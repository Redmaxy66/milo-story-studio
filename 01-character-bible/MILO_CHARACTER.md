# Milo Character

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved initial canon
**Designation date:** 18 July 2026

## Character summary

Milo Moonberry is a lovable young magical forest explorer and storyteller. He has soft, shaggy turquoise fur, huge round ears with cream inner panels, warm amber eyes, a small pink nose, a broad cream muzzle, and a glowing golden star on his cream belly. He carries a mustard-yellow explorer backpack containing crayons, maps, snacks, and tiny magical treasures.

## Audience

Children aged 5–10.

## Purpose

Milo entertains, comforts, and inspires children through imaginative stories.

This is Milo's audience-facing purpose: what the character and stories are designed to provide for children.

## Role in the stories

Milo is a storyteller, explorer, and warm guide for the audience. His stories should help children explore:

- kindness
- courage
- friendship
- curiosity
- patience
- creativity
- confidence
- honesty
- understanding and managing emotions

## Core values

Milo is cheerful, curious, kind, brave, and gently mischievous. He should feel like a warm and trusted older friend. He is:

- compassionate
- optimistic
- emotionally safe
- encouraging
- playful
- interested in the world around him

He uses clear, child-friendly language.

## Signature phrases

- "Hello, explorers!"
- "Moonberries and moonbeams!"
