# Milo Character Bible v1.0

**Status:** Approved initial canon
**Designation date:** 18 July 2026

This folder contains the approved initial Character Bible for Milo Moonberry.

## Included documents

- MILO_CHARACTER.md — central character overview
- MILO_BACKSTORY.md — approved origin and in-world purpose
- VOICE_GUIDE.md — voice, language, pacing, and tone
- PERSONALITY_RULES.md — approved traits and derived behavioural guidance
- VISUAL_REFERENCE.md — approved appearance and environmental consistency

## Information status

### Approved canon

Facts explicitly approved as part of Milo's identity, appearance, history,
voice, purpose, personality, and world.

### Derived production guidance

Writing and behavioural guidance logically derived from Milo's approved
traits. It should be followed for consistency but may be refined as approved
stories and examples develop.

### Open decisions

Details that have not yet been approved. They must not be treated as canon or
invented without an explicit decision.

## Change control

Future canonical changes must:

1. Be explicitly approved.
2. Be recorded in DECISION_LOG.md.
3. Update the relevant Character Bible document.
4. Update the version number when the change is material.
5. Be reviewed for consistency across all five documents.
