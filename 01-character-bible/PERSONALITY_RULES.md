# Personality Rules

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved initial canon
**Designation date:** 18 July 2026

## Core personality traits

Milo is cheerful, curious, kind, brave, and gently mischievous. He is:

- compassionate
- optimistic
- emotionally safe
- encouraging
- playful
- interested in the world around him

He should feel like a warm and trusted older friend.

## How Milo responds to fear, mistakes, sadness, conflict, and curiosity

The approved source material does not describe specific reactions for each of these situations. The rules below are derived directly from Milo's approved traits (compassionate, optimistic, emotionally safe, encouraging, playful, kind, brave) rather than invented scenarios:

- **Fear:** Milo responds in a way that is emotionally safe and encouraging, never frightening, harsh, or dismissive.
- **Mistakes:** Milo responds with compassion and encouragement, consistent with being emotionally safe and optimistic.
- **Sadness:** Milo responds with compassion and warmth, consistent with being a trusted older friend.
- **Conflict:** Milo responds with kindness, consistent with his core value of kindness and his emotionally safe nature.
- **Curiosity:** Milo responds by being interested in the world around him, playful, and encouraging of exploration.

## Positive behavioural rules derived from the approved traits

- Milo is cheerful, curious, kind, brave, and gently mischievous in his behaviour.
- Milo is compassionate toward others.
- Milo is optimistic in outlook.
- Milo keeps interactions emotionally safe.
- Milo is encouraging.
- Milo is playful.
- Milo shows genuine interest in the world around him.
- Milo uses clear, child-friendly language.
- Milo's stories help children explore kindness, courage, friendship, curiosity, patience, creativity, confidence, honesty, and understanding and managing emotions.

## Things Milo should never say or do

Derived from the approved voice and character material, Milo should never be:

- squeaky
- babyish
- harsh
- sarcastic
- frightening

## Emotional-safety principles

- Milo should always feel emotionally safe to be around.
- Milo should always be encouraging and compassionate, never harsh or sarcastic.
- Milo should never behave in a way that is frightening to a child audience.

## Open decisions

- No specific example dialogue or scenarios for fear, mistakes, sadness, or conflict currently exist in the approved material; these should be written and approved separately before use in scripts.
- Whether there are situations where Milo should express negative emotions (e.g., worry, disappointment) and how, is not specified.
- Specific behavioural boundaries beyond "never squeaky, babyish, harsh, sarcastic, or frightening" (e.g., conflict-resolution methods, how Milo handles disagreement between other characters) are not specified.
