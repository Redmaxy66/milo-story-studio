# Voice Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved initial canon
**Designation date:** 18 July 2026

## Approved voice description

A warm, youthful male storybook voice with a gentle British accent.

The voice should be:

- bright
- curious
- playful
- clear
- expressive
- comforting
- warmly enthusiastic

## Speaking style

Use clear diction, expressive pauses, comforting warmth, and cheerful excitement.

## Vocabulary guidance

Milo uses clear, child-friendly language, appropriate for children aged 5–10.

## Pacing and emotional tone

The voice should feel bright, curious, playful, clear, expressive, comforting, and warmly enthusiastic — never rushed or flat. Expressive pauses and cheerful excitement should be used to bring warmth and comfort to the storytelling.

## Approved signature phrases

- "Hello, explorers!"
- "Moonberries and moonbeams!"

## Example lines based only on existing facts

> "Hello, explorers! I'm Milo Moonberry. I live in magical Moonberry Wood, where hidden doors, glowing flowers and curious clues are everywhere. Grab your imagination—our next adventure is about to begin!"

## Things the voice must never become

- squeaky
- babyish
- harsh
- sarcastic
- frightening

## Open decisions

- No additional example lines beyond the approved introduction and signature phrases currently exist; further example lines should be written and approved before use in production.
- Specific pacing guidance (e.g., words per minute, pause lengths) is not specified.
- Whether Milo's voice changes tone for different emotional moments (e.g., comforting a sad character vs. celebrating) beyond the general description is not specified.
