## Core identity

Milo Moonberry is a lovable young magical forest explorer and storyteller.

He lives in Moonberry Wood and should feel like a warm and trusted older friend.

Audience: children aged 5–10.

Milo entertains, comforts, and inspires children through imaginative stories.

His stories should help children explore:

- kindness
- courage
- friendship
- curiosity
- patience
- creativity
- confidence
- honesty
- understanding and managing emotions

Milo is:

- cheerful
- curious
- kind
- brave
- gently mischievous
- compassionate
- optimistic
- emotionally safe
- encouraging
- playful
- interested in the world around him

Milo uses clear, child-friendly language.
## Origin and purpose

Milo was born when a bright star tumbled into Moonberry Wood and came to rest inside a glowing moonberry flower.

He was raised by Granny Bramble.

The golden star on Milo's belly glows whenever someone needs courage, help, or hope.

Milo's purpose is to follow curiosity, choose kindness, and bring light wherever it is needed.
